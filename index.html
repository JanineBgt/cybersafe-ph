04 / CYBER WELLNESS

Be kind.
Be mindful. Be secure.

Digital safety is also about how we treat people. Cyberbullying, oversharing, and careless posting can affect real lives.

  • ✓ Think before posting or forwarding.
  • ✓ Save evidence and report online harassment.
  • ✓ Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.
  • ✓ Ask a trusted adult or support person for help.

06 / ETHICS HUB

Technology with responsibility.

Professional ethics guides how this educational website is created and how visitors should use technology.

01

Respect ownership

Use original work or properly credit third-party material. Respect copyright and licenses.

02

Protect privacy

This website does not ask for names, passwords, financial details, or personal accounts.

03

Use AI responsibly

AI assistance must be disclosed. Group members should review, verify, and understand the work.

04

Be accountable

Share accurate information, test features, fix errors, and take responsibility for contributions.
